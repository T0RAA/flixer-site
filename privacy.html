Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Flixer ("the app") is a personal tool that edits short video clips and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel (@FlixerrYT) and TikTok account (@flixxerr1). This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by its operator. It does not offer accounts, sign-in, or any service to the public, and it does not collect personal information from viewers, visitors to this website, or other users of YouTube or TikTok.

Data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: with the operator's consent through Google OAuth, the app uses the scopes youtube.upload and youtube to upload videos, set thumbnails, and read basic details (channel name and handle, upload and processing status) of the operator's own channel.
  • TikTok: with the operator's consent through TikTok Login Kit, the app uses user.info.basic (display name, avatar, open ID), video.upload, and video.publish to confirm the connected account and send videos to that account.
  • Public videos: the app reads publicly available metadata (title, channel, view count, license) of public YouTube videos to find content that is licensed for reuse.

How data is used

Account data and access tokens are used only to upload the operator's own videos and to check that uploads succeeded. They are not used for advertising, profiling, analytics about other people, or training machine-learning models.

Storage and security

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, in files readable only by the operator's user account. No data is stored on a separate server, and this website stores nothing about its visitors.

Sharing

Data obtained from Google or TikTok is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party. It is transferred only to Google and TikTok themselves, as part of the upload requests.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the operator disconnects the app. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions or in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working immediately; the operator also deletes the token files when disconnecting the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: open a request on GitHub.
