Short clips, full credit.

Flixer is a small, personal publishing tool. It finds videos that are licensed for reuse (Creative Commons) or that creators have given permission to clip, edits the best moments into vertical shorts with captions, and posts them to Flixer's own channels, always crediting and linking the original creator.

Where to watch

  • YouTube: @FlixerrYT
  • TikTok: @flixxerr1

How it uses YouTube and TikTok

Flixer signs in only to its operator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts and uses the official YouTube Data API and TikTok Content Posting API to upload videos to those accounts. It does not offer sign-in to the public and does not collect data from viewers. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Creators

If you are the creator of a clipped video and want it removed or credited differently, open a request and it will be handled promptly.
